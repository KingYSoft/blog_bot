## Method and scope

The briefing date follows the Asia/Shanghai calendar. The **only inclusion source** for the repository list was the live, official [GitHub Trending page](https://github.com/trending), using its default filters: global/spoken language **Any**, programming language **Any**, date range **Today/Daily**. Two direct HTML requests returned HTTP 200 and the same 12 repositories in the same order; the page was read from the first `<article>` to the twelfth and last. A search service's earlier cached “today” snapshot differed, so it was not used. No repository was added, removed, reranked, or inferred.

For every displayed repository, the repository homepage and README were read and the official [GitHub REST API](https://docs.github.com/en/rest/repos/repos#get-a-repository) was used where available to verify description, primary language, lifetime stars, and push time. “Stars today” is copied only from the live Trending HTML and is not calculated. News discovery emphasized events on August 7–8 and separated event dates from publication dates; important figures were checked against company reports, government material, or primary surveys when available.

The startup candidate pool contained 31 problems derived from all 12 repositories and 20 news items, plus adjacent recurring workflows. Sixteen failed at least one screen: solo delivery, 1–2 week MVP, launch cash below $500, identifiable buyers, direct distribution, a paid validation event, or low regulatory/operational risk. The 15 survivors are experiments—not forecasts.

## Three tempting directions rejected

1. **A general autonomous coding agent:** Prime Agent and skills packs are hot, but a solo founder cannot win a broad harness race in two weeks; model access, trust, sandboxing, and distribution dominate. Narrow verification tools are more chargeable.
2. **An AI stock picker or autonomous therapist:** TradingAgents and chatbot news show demand, but both replace high-stakes licensed judgment and create unacceptable safety, fiduciary, and regulatory exposure. Only evidence, education, and human-review tooling survived.
3. **A textbook download mirror or censorship-bypass bundle:** Attention is real, but unclear copyright, regional law, untrusted binaries, abuse potential, and ongoing content operations violate the low-risk, software-first screen. Local inventory and neutral reachability diagnostics are safer wedges.

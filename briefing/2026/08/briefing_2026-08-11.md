## Method

The sole repository inclusion source was the live, official [GitHub Trending](https://github.com/trending) page under its default filters: **global, any spoken language, any programming language, Today/Daily**. I read the visible page from its heading to the footer and repeated the extraction; both passes returned the same 16 repositories in the same order. No cached list, search result, third-party ranking, or extra repository was used. Every repository homepage and README was then read, while lifetime stars, primary language, license and push time were cross-checked through the official GitHub API. Lifetime stars can move after the stated capture time.

News selection separated the underlying event date from publication time. Because 11:19 CST is still late August 10 in the Americas, the pool includes material August 10 releases plus consequential August 6–7 developments that remained current. Large figures were cross-checked against company releases or a second authoritative report where available; proposals and memoranda are labeled as such. Startup ideas were screened from the complete 16-repository list and 20-story news pool, then constrained to one full-stack founder, a 1–2 week MVP, normally under $500 cash cost, direct online distribution, and a paid-validation threshold.

## 15 startup experiments for a solo software founder

**Screening result.** The candidate pool started with 34 concepts derived from all 16 Trending repositories and 20 news stories. The 15 below survived the build-time, cash-cost, buyer specificity, direct-distribution, risk and paid-validation filters. Scores are **solo feasibility + willingness to pay + ease of acquisition + developer fit**, each 1–5, ordered by total and then narrower buyer/shorter proof cycle.

### 1. AI account anomaly proxy for small software teams — **19/20 (5+5+4+5)**

- **Positioning / pain / evidence:** A local or VPC proxy that flags stolen AI credentials, token bursts and agents acting outside approved hours or endpoints. Today's Black Hat report cited an 89% attack increase and a 200,000-request burst; `paperclip` and `prime-agent` show agents running continuously. Alternatives are raw provider bills, SIEM rules and manual key rotation.
- **Customer / first 20:** CTOs or security-minded platform leads at 10–100 person SaaS companies using at least two of OpenAI, Anthropic and Gemini. Find 20 through public AI-engineering job posts, small SaaS security communities and Black Hat discussion threads; first outreach: send a screenshot of a synthetic “stolen key” timeline and ask for a 15-minute log-schema review.
- **Solution / leverage / MVP:** OpenAI-compatible reverse proxy, usage baselines, rule-based anomaly alerts, key-to-owner map and signed incident export. Use LiteLLM/OpenTelemetry, SQLite/Postgres and Slack webhooks. **Build only:** proxy, three detectors, dashboard, alert and export. **Do not build:** full SIEM, automatic account shutdown, threat attribution or endpoint agent.
- **Business / channels:** $49/team/month for five keys; $149 onboarding for a private deployment. Open-source the log schema and publish two reproducible LLMjacking detections, then contact 20 platform leads directly.

## Three popular-looking directions rejected

1. **A general “AI company of agents.”** `paperclip`, `agency-agents` and `prime-agent` make the category look irresistible, but a clone has no narrow buyer, inherits arbitrary-code and credential risk, and needs broad integrations. The viable wedges are provenance, budget controls and regression tests.
2. **Autonomous AI trading or investment advice.** `TradingAgents` and volatile macro headlines prove interest, not safe product-market fit. Data licensing, look-ahead bias, non-determinism, regulation and liability violate the two-week low-risk constraint; audit tooling is the safer sell-the-picks layer.
3. **Consumer Wi-Fi health monitoring or an iOS jailbreak marketplace.** RuView's medical-adjacent claims require hardware calibration and careful validation, while Dopamine distribution adds device-bricking, malware-trust and platform/legal risk. A solo MVP should stay with non-medical calibration evidence, not diagnosis or exploit distribution.
